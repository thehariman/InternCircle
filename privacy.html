Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 9, 2026

At InternCircle, we are committed to safeguarding your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and protect your personal information when you register for our virtual internships or visit our platform.

1. Information We Collect

When you register on InternCircle, we collect specific personal details necessary to enroll you in our internship programs. This information includes:

  • Personal Identity: Your full name, college/university, course of study, and current year.
  • Contact Details: Your email address and 10-digit mobile number.
  • Location & Preferences: Your state of residence and your preferred internship domain track.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process your personal data solely for program management and verification purposes, including:

  • Dispatching your internship offer letters and coordinator updates.
  • Verifying student credentials and certificate authenticity via the public database matching.
  • Responding to customer support inquiries and student questions.

3. Zero Spam Policy

We believe in absolute data respect. We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party advertisers. You will never receive spam advertisements, promotion calls, or marketing texts from us or external partners.

4. Data Security

Your details are stored securely. We implement industry-standard database access controls, server firewalls, and encryption protocols to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Third-Party Sharing & Verification

We only share data in the following restricted scenarios:

  • Recruiter Verification: Employers can search your Certificate ID on our database to verify your completion status and grade. No detailed contact metrics (like mobile numbers) are publicly shared during this verification process.
  • Compliance with Law: We may disclose data if required to do so by legal authorities in compliance with applicable local regulations.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our platform uses standard tracking cookies to maintain secure user sessions (such as keeping you logged in to your student dashboard) and gather aggregate, non-personal analytical statistics on site traffic to help us improve user experience.

7. Your Rights & Access

You have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections to any inaccurate details, or request the deletion of your registration profile. To make any inquiries, please contact our support desk.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at:

Email: support@interncircle.com
