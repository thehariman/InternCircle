How to Add Internship to Your Resume

Your internship should be listed under the "Internship Experience" or "Work Experience" section of your resume. If you have no prior work experience, this section becomes the most important part of your resume â€” don't underestimate it.

How to Add Your Certificate to LinkedIn (Step by Step)

1

Go to Your LinkedIn Profile

Log in at linkedin.com and click on your name/profile picture to open your profile page.

2

Click "Add profile section"

Below your headline and introduction card, click the blue "Add profile section" button.

3

Select "Licenses & Certifications"

Under "Recommended" in the dropdown, choose "Licenses & Certifications". This is the correct section for your internship certificate.

4

Fill in the Certificate Details

Name: Web Development Internship Certificate
Issuing Organisation: InternCircle
Issue Date: Month & Year you received it
Credential ID: Your certificate ID (from the certificate PDF)
Credential URL: Link to interncircle.in (or QR verification link if provided)

5

Also Add the "Experience" Section Entry

Separately, click "Add profile section" again and choose "Work Experience". Add InternCircle as employer, "Web Development Intern" as title, and list your responsibilities as bullet points â€” same as your resume entry.

6

Share a Post About Your Achievement

After saving, write a short post sharing your certificate on LinkedIn. Tag InternCircle and use hashtags like #internship #freeinternship #webdevelopment. This gets you profile views and endorsements from your network.

Keywords to Include on Your Resume and LinkedIn

Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan for keywords. Include these relevant terms in your profile and resume:

  • HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (specific technologies you used)
  • Responsive Web Design / Mobile-First Design
  • Virtual Internship / Remote Internship
  • Verified Certificate of Completion
  • Letter of Recommendation
  • Hands-on Project-Based Learning
  • Frontend Development

Frequently Asked Questions

Is an online internship certificate valuable on a resume?
Yes â€” especially when it's from a verified platform and includes a QR-authenticated completion certificate. Recruiters understand that remote/virtual internships are now standard, and a verified certificate demonstrates real commitment and skill-building.
Where exactly should I put the internship on my resume?
Create a dedicated "Internship Experience" or "Work Experience" section. If you have other work experience, put this internship chronologically in the same section. If this is your only experience, make it the first major section after your education.
How do I add the LOR (Letter of Recommendation) to my resume?
The LOR is typically attached as a separate document when applying for jobs (if requested). On your resume, you can simply note "LOR Available" at the end of your internship entry. On LinkedIn, upload it as a Featured media item on your profile.
Can I list multiple InternCircle internships on my resume?
Yes. If you completed internships in more than one domain (e.g., Web Development and Data Science), list them as separate entries in your Experience section. Having multiple certificates demonstrates range and initiative.
