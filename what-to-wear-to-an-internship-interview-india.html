What to Wear to an Internship Interview in India (2026 Dress Code Guide)

📅 September 18, 2026 🕒 5 min read 👥 By InternCircle Team

First impressions are formed in the first 7 seconds. Before you say a single word in your internship interview, your appearance has already communicated something about you. Dressing appropriately shows professionalism, respect for the opportunity, and self-awareness.

Here is a practical, India-specific guide on exactly what to wear to your internship interview, broken down by industry and format.

💡 Golden Rule: When in doubt, dress one level more formally than you think the company requires. It is much better to be overdressed than underdressed.

Understanding Indian Workplace Dress Codes

Indian companies broadly fall into three dress culture categories:

  • Formal: Banks, consulting firms, law firms, large corporations — expect business formals
  • Business Casual: Mid-size companies, marketing agencies, most IT companies — smart but not overly formal
  • Smart Casual: Startups, creative agencies, some tech product companies — clean, modern, and polished

What to Wear: Men

For Formal/Corporate Interviews (Banking, Finance, Consulting)

  • Light-coloured formal shirt (white, light blue, or pastel) — well-ironed
  • Dark formal trousers (navy, charcoal, or black)
  • Formal leather shoes — polished
  • Optional: A solid-coloured tie and blazer for senior-level company interviews
  • Avoid: Loud patterns, bright colours, casual sneakers, or jeans

For Business Casual/Tech Interviews (IT Companies, Product Startups)

  • A clean, well-fitted shirt or polo (solid colours or subtle patterns)
  • Chinos or dark jeans (no rips or distressing)
  • Clean leather or canvas shoes — no flip-flops
  • Optional: A well-fitted blazer adds instant professionalism

What to Wear: Women

For Formal/Corporate Interviews

  • Formal saree, salwar kameez, or Western formal suit — all are equally professional
  • For Western: A formal blouse/shirt with trousers or a formal knee-length skirt
  • Closed-toe formal shoes or heels (comfortable ones — you don't want to be distracted)
  • Keep jewellery minimal and understated
  • Avoid: Heavily embellished clothing, very casual western wear, or overly bright colours

For Business Casual/Tech Interviews

  • A well-fitted kurta, formal blouse, or professional western top
  • Formal trousers, formal skirt, or well-pressed palazzos
  • Clean, comfortable footwear — formal flats work perfectly

Virtual Interview Dress Code

Even for a virtual interview, dress professionally from the waist up. The camera shows everything above your desk. Avoid:

  • Busy patterns that look strange on camera
  • Pure white (can cause overexposure on webcam)
  • Very shiny jewellery that catches light and distracts

Solid, medium-toned colours (navy, teal, medium blue, burgundy) work best on camera.

Grooming: The Often Overlooked Part

  • Hair: Clean, neat, and away from your face
  • Nails: Clean and trimmed
  • Fragrance: Subtle — not overpowering
  • Bags/accessories: Professional-looking and not excessive
